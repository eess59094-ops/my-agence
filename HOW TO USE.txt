=====================================================
  AI COMPANY HQ — HOW TO USE
=====================================================

START (10 seconds):
  1. Open this folder on your Desktop (AI-Company-HQ)
  2. Double-click  index.html
     -> it opens in your browser and starts running by itself.
  No install, no node.js, no internet needed. It is fully offline.

-----------------------------------------------------
WHAT YOU ARE LOOKING AT
-----------------------------------------------------
It's your own AI content company: 1 CEO + 14 employees,
split into 4 groups (divisions), that boot up instantly:

  📡 GROUP 1 — TREND DIVISION
     Searches for what is trending right now, audits rival
     channels, finds keywords + niches, writes the script/hook.
     THEY LOCK THE NEXT VIDEO'S CONCEPT.

  🎬 GROUP 2 — STUDIO DIVISION
     Builds the video with AI: AI video generation, advanced
     editing, sound design, motion graphics, thumbnails.
     EVERY CLIP THEY PUSH MOVES THE PIPELINE FORWARD.

  📣 GROUP 3 — DISTRIBUTION DIVISION
     Publishes the finished video to your YouTube channel,
     cuts it into shorts, and schedules it on other apps with
     native captions. Also reads the analytics after launch.

  🎓 GROUP 4 — SKILLS & QA DIVISION
     Fact-checks every claim before publish, hunts problems
     in the workflow, and runs daily DRILLS so everyone's
     SKILLS LEVE UP. This group makes the whole company grow.

-----------------------------------------------------
THE 5 TABS
-----------------------------------------------------
  🛋 OFFICE BOARD  — live desk view. Every employee's status,
     current task + progress bar, and the LIVE FEED of chatter,
     shipping reports, drama and coffee runs.
  👥 TEAM & LEVELS — everyone's level, XP bar, morale, top 6
     skills with L0-L5 pips, and their office bonds.
  📚 SKILLS LIBRARY — the 20 skills of the company, who owns
     them, average level, and the "top reps this week" panel.
  📋 MEETINGS — full minutes of every 8:00 brief and every
     Sunday all-hands (promotions!), plus channel totals.
  🚀 PROJECTS — the video pipeline: which stage the current
     video is in, the LIVE video and its view count, plus
     your published-video history.

-----------------------------------------------------
HOW THE DAY WORKS
-----------------------------------------------------
  08:00  — daily brief (CEO + 4 division leads set the day's play)
  09:00-18:00 — work. employees grind tasks that push the video
          through the pipeline: Trend -> Script -> AI Generation ->
          Edit & GFX -> QA -> Publish
  SUNDAY 10:00 — the big weekly meeting: views report, best video,
          skill report, QA flags, and PROMOTIONS. Someone levels up
          almost every week.
  When a video publishes it goes LIVE and starts pulling views.
  Viral-grade / solid / slow-burn verdicts depend on QA depth
  and office vibe — keep morale up and the office happy.

-----------------------------------------------------
CONTROLS (top bar)
-----------------------------------------------------
  ⏸ pause           — freeze the simulation
  1x / 3x / 8x      — time speed
  ⏭ Skip to meeting — fast-forward straight to the next
     brief or Sunday meeting
  ⟲ Reset company   — wipes the save and restarts fresh
  Click the X on a live meeting modal to close it — the full
  minutes are always saved in the MEETINGS tab.

-----------------------------------------------------
AUTO-SAVE & CATCH-UP
-----------------------------------------------------
  The company auto-saves every 10 seconds in your browser
  and closes the tab saves too.
  When you reopen index.html after being away, the company
  FAST-FORWARDS through everything you missed — videos
  published, views earned, Sunday minutes waiting in the
  Meetings tab — then returns to live time.
  Honest note: while your PC is fully OFF, nothing can run
  on it (that's physics, not a limitation of this app).
  The catch-up mode is the workaround: the office picks up
  from exactly where you left it, using the real clock.
  If the save ever seems missing, it may be your browser
  blocking storage for local files — running via
  "npx serve" fixes that (see README).
